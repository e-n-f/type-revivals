# Scans

The sources for the Executive revivals, kept here so that the fonts can be rebuilt from them. The
*Typesetting* pages are screenshots, which nobody could make again pixel for pixel, and the build's text-block
positions are measured on these files.

The face behind Jowa's and the Science Museum's "602" is IBM's Modern: the same letterforms as the 1951 Model A
sample, which names its face, and as the Finnish brochure's "Proportional Modern" (see Fonts In Use,
[IBM Executive Modern](https://fontsinuse.com/typefaces/114538/ibm-executive-modern)).

Also consulted, but too small to trace: IBM's 1960 catalogue pages 19–21, the IBM UK "type styles" brochure (1968,
eBay photographs; its "1/32 inch unit" and "1/45 inch unit" headings confirm the fitted escapements), and IBM's Mag
Card Executive element sheets. An AI-upscaled copy of the UK brochure is not used for tracing.

Also consulted: the IBM electric typewriter catalogue of 1960, pages 19–21, from [Typecast](https://typecast.munk.org/2011/05/11/the-ibm-electric-typewriter-catalog-1960/), whose typed samples give each face's escapement. They agree with the fitted ones (1/36 inch for Mid-Century and Bold Face No. 2, 1/45 for Charter, 1/32 for the others).
